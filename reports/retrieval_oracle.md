## 4. Retrieval — §A.7 measured against the gold sets

Backend: `lexical`.

> **These are instrument checks, not method measurements.** The backend is a token-overlap stand-in for §A.4's `text-embedding-3-small`, so the absolute values say nothing about OKH-RAG's quality. Their use is the *comparison between the three rows below*: the shuffle row holds retrieved content fixed by construction, so recall must not move while the order metrics must.

| configuration | pool recall | anchor recall | trajectory recall | chance recall | over chance | first gold step | canonical concordance | gold concordance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| heuristic | 0.5782 | 0.2231 | 0.0319 | 0.0308 | 1.036 | 3.1 | 0.6783 | None |
| no_order | 0.5782 | 0.2231 | 0.0312 | 0.0308 | 1.013 | 3.375 | 0.541 | 0.3333 |
| heuristic_shuffled | 0.5782 | 0.2231 | 0.0319 | 0.0308 | 1.036 | 5.7 | 0.4379 | None |

`chance recall` is `pool recall × L / |C|` — what a trajectory of the same length drawn uniformly from the pool would score. It is the floor `trajectory recall` has to be read against: the two numbers are the same to two decimals on this backend, which says the beam is selecting gold at chance and that the absolute recall is a statement about the embedder, not about §A.7. `over chance` is the ratio, and 1.0 means chance.

`canonical concordance` is the agreement between the retrieved sequence and §A.3's canonical order over all its placed members — 1.0 is perfect, and shuffling `L` members gives 0.5 in expectation, so it is the metric §4.5's shuffle row must move. `gold concordance` restricts the same comparison to the gold members and is `None` on most records at realistic recall; its denominator (`_n`) is in the JSON.
